<h2>Limits</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Paragraphs only, 1 to 20.</strong> There is no option to ask for a number of words, sentences or characters. To fill a field with a character limit, generate one paragraph and trim it; the <a href="/tools/word-counter/">word counter</a> shows the length.</li>
  <li><strong>Values outside the range are clamped.</strong> Typing 50 gives 20 paragraphs; 0 or an empty field gives 5, the default.</li>
  <li><strong>Latin only.</strong> Word and line lengths match Latin-script languages roughly. Chinese, Japanese and Korean text wraps and breaks lines differently, so test those layouts with real text in the target language.</li>
  <li><strong>Not random enough for test data.</strong> Sentences come from a short list and repeat across paragraphs. For realistic names, emails or addresses, use the <a href="/tools/fake-data-generator/">fake data generator</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>No headings or lists.</strong> Output is flat paragraphs; add your own headings to check heading spacing.</li>
</ul>

<h2>What is Lorem Ipsum?</h2>
<p>
  Lorem Ipsum is the usual placeholder text in typesetting and design. It is a cut and scrambled passage from
  Cicero's philosophical work <em>De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum</em> (45 BC), sections 1.10.32–1.10.33; Richard McClintock, a
  Latin professor at Hampden-Sydney College, is credited with tracing the source. The popular story that a printer used it for
  a type specimen book in the 1500s has no surviving specimen behind it. Philippe Cibois dates it between the 1914 Loeb
  edition of Cicero and the Letraset dry-transfer sheets of the 1960s; the oldest specimen he could examine is a 1970s
  Letraset brochure. Desktop publishing software spread it further.
</p>
<p>
  Its words have realistic lengths for a Latin-script language, so it fills lines and paragraphs
  plausibly without the distraction of meaningful content. It is not English, though: the standard paragraph has no
  j, k, w, y or z and almost no h, so check glyphs and line breaks with real text as well.
</p>
